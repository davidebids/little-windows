# App Store Campaign Notes

## Recommended iPhone order

1. **One less thing to hold in your head.** Emotional promise and broad product positioning.
2. **Know what needs you next.** Workflow proof through the real attention queue.
3. **Patterns you can actually use.** Confidence builder through a real seven-day report.
4. **First foods, without the fog.** Specialized child-care depth.
5. **The list that thinks ahead.** Household coordination across inventory, shopping, and meal prep.
6. **Keep night care gentle.** Memorable closing benefit and night-light experience.

The first three are designed as a miniature sales page: promise, proof, confidence. Copy stays benefit-led and short enough to survive thumbnail presentation. The interface crops remain authentic simulator output; the surrounding imagery provides the emotional campaign layer.

## Recommended Apple Watch order

1. **The day, at a glance.**
2. **Timers stay close.**
3. **Medication stays close.**

## Recommended 13-inch iPad order

1. **One less thing to hold in your head.** Broad product promise with the native tablet Today workspace.
2. **Know what needs you next.** A six-item care and household attention queue.
3. **Patterns you can actually use.** Seven-day sleep reporting and week-over-week context.
4. **First foods, without the fog.** Food planning, tracking, allergens, recipes, and safety guidance.
5. **The list that thinks ahead.** Shopping suggestions connected to inventory and meal prep.
6. **Keep night care gentle.** A warm, low-distraction night-light experience with quick controls.

## Generated campaign imagery

The three background artworks were created with the built-in image-generation workflow and then combined with untouched simulator UI captures.

- **Night indigo:** A sophisticated abstract editorial backdrop evoking quiet caregiving and relief, using deep indigo, translucent window-like apertures, moonlit light, and subtle tactile grain; no text, people, devices, logos, or UI.
- **Daylight order:** A calm abstract study of household order using moonlit cream, blush peach, sea-glass teal, frosted-glass planes, and daylight; no text, people, devices, logos, or UI.
- **Night amber:** A refined abstract nighttime backdrop using espresso, burgundy, amber light, translucent windows, and smoked-glass depth; no text, people, devices, logos, or UI.
