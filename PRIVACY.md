# Privacy Policy for Little Windows

**Effective date: August 16, 2026**

**Last updated: August 16, 2026**

Little Windows is a local-first home organizer and optional care tracker for children, adults, and dogs. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Little Windows app, its widgets, Live Activities, shortcuts, and Apple Watch companion (collectively, **Little Windows**).

## Summary

- Your Little Windows data is stored on your device unless you choose an iCloud-backed sync mode or export or share information yourself.
- Little Windows does not send your care data to the developer and does not use a developer-operated app server.
- Little Windows does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.
- Optional Apple services—including iCloud, CloudKit sharing, Maps, WeatherKit, notifications, widgets, Live Activities, and Watch connectivity—process limited information needed to provide the features you choose.
- You control data export, Family Sync participation, and deletion from within the app.

## Information You May Enter

Depending on the features you use, Little Windows may store information such as:

- Profile details, including a name, profile type, birth or adoption date, relationship, notes, profile photo, and optional child, adult, or dog details
- Care history, routines, timers, feeding, sleep, diapering, potty, activity, growth, symptoms, temperature, medications, dose history, appointments, milestones, memories, and other notes or photos
- Adult health observations that you enter, such as blood pressure, pulse, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, blood glucose, pain, sleep, activity, weight, and temperature
- Household information, including to-do lists, shopping lists, inventory, meals, returns, trip plans, destinations, itineraries, packing lists, assignments, links, and reminders
- App preferences, notification settings, guide progress, prediction records, caregiver labels, sync state, and diagnostics shown within the app

You decide what information to enter. Little Windows is intended to be used by an adult caregiver or household member. Avoid entering information you do not have permission to store or share.

## How Information Is Used

Little Windows processes information to provide app features, including:

- Displaying, organizing, editing, and searching your records
- Running timers and calculating summaries, charts, trends, and pattern-based planning aids on the device
- Scheduling local notifications that you request
- Providing widgets, Live Activities, App Intents, shortcuts, deep links, and Apple Watch features
- Creating backups and care reports when you request them
- Syncing data through Apple services when you enable an iCloud-backed mode
- Searching for destinations and providing forecasts and weather-based packing suggestions when you use those trip features

Little Windows does not use your care data for advertising or sell it.

## Storage and iCloud Sync

Little Windows offers the following data modes:

### Local only

The main Little Windows data stays in the app's storage on the current device. The operating system may include app data in device backups according to your device and Apple backup settings.

### Private iCloud Sync

If enabled, Little Windows uses Apple's CloudKit service to sync app data through the private iCloud database associated with your Apple Account. This mode is intended for your devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

### Family Sync

If enabled, Family Sync uses Apple's CloudKit sharing features. Accepted caregivers on different Apple Accounts can access the shared household data. Care profiles are private by default and are included only when their owner explicitly opts them in. People with access to a Family Sync space may be able to view, change, export, or retain copies of shared information.

Family Sync is separate from Apple Family Sharing membership. Review the people who have access before sharing sensitive information. Removing someone from a share stops future access through that share, but it cannot remove copies they previously exported or saved.

Apple processes information used by iCloud and CloudKit under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Apple Maps and WeatherKit

When you search for a trip destination, your search text is sent to Apple Maps to return matching places. When you request trip weather, the selected destination coordinates and dates needed for the forecast are processed through Apple's WeatherKit service.

Little Windows does not request or use your device's current geographic location. Saved destinations and forecast results may be included in your local data, iCloud sync, Family Sync, and backups according to the choices you make.

## Photos

If you choose to add a photo, Apple's system photo picker gives Little Windows access only to the item or items you select. Selected photos are copied into Little Windows data and follow the same local storage, sync, sharing, backup, export, and deletion choices as the record to which they are attached.

## Notifications and System Surfaces

If you allow notifications, Little Windows schedules reminders and status updates through Apple's notification system. Notification content may be visible on the Lock Screen or other system surfaces according to your device settings.

Widgets, Live Activities, App Intents, shortcuts, and Control Center controls use limited app-group snapshots and commands needed to display or perform the feature. The Apple Watch companion receives limited profile, timer, medication, prediction, favorite-action, and summary information needed for its features through Apple's Watch connectivity system. These surfaces do not directly edit the full Little Windows data store.

You can change notification visibility and permissions in the Settings app on your device, and you can remove widgets or disable related features at any time.

## Exports and Links

When you create a JSON backup, CSV report, PDF report, or other export, Little Windows gives the file to the system share or file interface you choose. After export, the file is outside Little Windows' control. It may contain sensitive personal or care information. You are responsible for storing it securely and sharing it only with people and services you trust.

Little Windows may open websites or map directions that you choose. Those third-party sites and services receive information under their own privacy policies. Little Windows does not control their practices.

## Information the Developer Receives

The developer does not receive the profiles, care history, health observations, household data, photos, backups, or reports that you store in Little Windows unless you deliberately send information while requesting support.

If you contact support, the developer receives the information you provide, such as your email address, message, and any attachments. Support information is used to respond to your request, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse, and keep necessary records. Do not send a full backup, care report, names, health information, photos, or other sensitive information unless support specifically asks for the minimum information needed to investigate a problem.

If you view this policy or request support through GitHub, GitHub may process technical and account information as described in the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Retention and Deletion

Data remains on your device until you edit or delete it, reset data within Little Windows, or remove the app, subject to device backups and automatic local recovery copies created by Little Windows during certain import, reset, or recovery operations.

Little Windows provides data controls under **Settings → Data**. The app explains the affected scope before an import or deletion is confirmed. Depending on the active mode, a change may affect the current device, devices using Private iCloud Sync, or accepted caregivers in a Family Sync space. Only the Family Sync owner can replace or erase the complete shared dataset through Little Windows.

Cloud data is subject to sync availability and Apple's retention and backup practices. Deleting the app from one device may not delete data stored in iCloud, on another device, in another caregiver's retained copy, or in files previously exported. Before deleting or replacing data, create a current backup if you may need to recover it.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse, unless a longer period is required by law.

## Children's Privacy

Little Windows is intended for use by adult caregivers and household members, not for use by children themselves. The app may contain information about a child when an adult chooses to enter it. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through Little Windows. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to support, contact the developer so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

## Security

Little Windows relies on protections provided by iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, and Apple services, as well as the access controls on your devices and Apple Account. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Use a device passcode, protect your Apple Account, review Family Sync participants, and handle exported files carefully.

## Medical and Veterinary Information

Little Windows is an organizational tool. It does not diagnose conditions, validate medications, or provide medical or veterinary advice. Contact an appropriate qualified professional about health decisions or emergencies.
